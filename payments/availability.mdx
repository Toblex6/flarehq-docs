## Boundaries to preserve

- **The customer pays directly to the merchant wallet; FlareHQ never holds checkout funds.** Circle Developer-Controlled treasury wallets still use server-authorized signing; do not describe every wallet operation as non-custodial.
- Circle SCAs cannot be x402 payers. The compatible payment EOA and SCA treasury are different wallets.
- `withGateway()` settles before its resource handler. A successful settlement does not guarantee upstream delivery, and the paid marketplace wrapper can return HTTP 200 with `upstreamOk: false`.
- Criterion-stream close pays the remaining budget to the **worker**, not a poster refund. Old per-second APIs are retired.
- No custom SwapPool was seeded. Invoice checkout routing and separate Flow swaps are not interchangeable.
- Outbound webhooks are unsigned/best-effort, with no durable retry queue. Cached status or zero-filled error balances are not live chain proof.

## Remaining owner steps

1. Reload Browser Control if needed and attach the existing checkout and merchant tabs through its toolbar button. The single-payment approval above is already recorded; do not send a PIN, code or private key to the assistant.
2. Recheck the exact signer, Arc `5042`, live balance, frozen recipient and invoice expiry before paying. Cached `PENDING` does not mean unexpired. First establish a **different unpaid invoice** with matching recipient/token/amount through the authenticated merchant flow; never pay that replay target.
3. Review fresh gas and all fees. Keep the test within **1 USDC** and the run within **3 USDC**. Stop at every PIN, code or signature prompt for the owner to act. Only the approved checkout may be submitted once.
4. Immediately reconcile the Arc Explorer receipt, signer after-balance, merchant credit at fixed blocks and read-only `PaymentLog`/`DirectPaymentProof` records. Inspect the separate **0.000025-USDC fee** and its row/receipt. Stop if settlement is uncertain; report any fee failure and wait for explicit OK before retrying.
5. Only after settlement reconciliation, reuse the proof against the **different unpaid invoice** and verify rejection with unchanged invoice/proof/fee state. Never use the paid invoice to probe replay or fee recovery, and never submit a second transfer.
6. Base→Arc remains a separate [bridge prerequisites](/payments/cctp-bridge) assessment, not an authorized bridge. Render inspection and Telegram still require owner login. No bridge, deployment, Safe action, sweep or configuration change is part of this continuation.

The latest production `/api/network` and `/api/cctp/transfer` GETs returned `200`; earlier `503` probes do not describe the latest snapshot. This is point-in-time availability, not an uptime guarantee. Read-only production-created rows matched the approved Neon database, but exact deployed Render settings remain uninspected behind sign-in.
