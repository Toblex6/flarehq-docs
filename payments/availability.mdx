FlareHQ production runs on **Arc Mainnet**, chain `5042`, CCTP domain `26`. Arc Testnet, chain `5042002`, is a separate development environment. Mainnet uses real funds.

## Live on Arc Mainnet

- **Checkout and payment links** — hosted and embedded checkout, merchant payment links and single-invoice references settle USDC directly from the customer to the merchant wallet. FlareHQ never holds checkout funds.
- **Platform fees** — a successful checkout payment is followed by a separate 25 bps (0.25%) merchant fee debit. The fee is its own recorded operation, not part of the customer's payment.
- **ArcFlareStream** — the criterion-tranche stream contract is deployed on Arc Mainnet at `0x79230A415dF3B22cD8c7DDB9b7Cd1f860e95375e`. Opening a stream still requires a funded ERC-8183 job, so job streams cannot be used while ERC-8183 jobs are unavailable.

## Not live or not yet proven

- **CCTP bridge** — the browser flow is configured-unverified and can list Base and Arc, but no completed mainnet bridge has been verified. The Circle server-authorized path returns `503` because its installed adapter lacks Arc Mainnet support. Treat bridging as unproven until a source burn, Iris attestation and Arc mint are reconciled.
- **ERC-8183 jobs** — no mainnet protocol address is configured. Hiring, funding, submission and completion are unavailable.
- **Escrow** — unavailable pending deployment and verification. A testnet address is never valid on mainnet.
- **x402** — disabled on mainnet, even when a verifier address is configured.
- **Payroll, scheduled payments and swaps** — not verified end to end on mainnet.

A route, an offline test, a listed endpoint or a visible listing does not prove mainnet settlement. Reconcile the receipt, parties, token, amount and recipient credit before fulfilling a payment or claiming a transfer.
