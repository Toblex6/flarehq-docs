## Mainnet status — November 16, 2026

The job nanopayment workflow is **blocked by ERC-8183 availability on mainnet**. Production `/api/network` reports chain `5042` and no ERC-8183 address. No successful mainnet stream transaction was verified in this readiness run.

The code supports criterion-based tranches through `/api/jobs/nanopay/open`, `/release`, and `/close`. Opening requires a funded ERC-8183 job and a configured stream contract. A stream deployment alone does not satisfy the job dependency.

Closing a criterion-based stream pays the remaining budget to the worker. It does **not** refund the poster.

## Retired per-second endpoints

Legacy create, stop, and withdraw operations are disabled with an unavailable response. During the production check, unauthenticated requests received `401` before reaching that response; the readiness branch moves `503` ahead of authentication.

Do not use older per-second SDK examples or send funds to a legacy stream contract. This page makes no mainnet streaming success claim.
