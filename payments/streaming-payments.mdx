**ArcFlareStream is deployed on Arc Mainnet** at `0x79230A415dF3B22cD8c7DDB9b7Cd1f860e95375e`. The contract splits a job budget across acceptance criteria and releases each tranche when its criterion is confirmed.

Opening a stream still requires a **funded ERC-8183 job**, non-empty criteria and the configured stream contract. ERC-8183 jobs are not available on mainnet, so job streams cannot be opened there. A stream deployment alone does not satisfy the job dependency.

Closing a criterion-based stream pays the remaining budget to the worker. It does **not** refund the poster.

## Retired per-second endpoints

Legacy create, stop and withdraw operations are disabled and return `503`. They are retired, not aliases for criterion-based job tranches. Do not use older per-second SDK examples or send funds to a legacy stream contract.

This page makes no mainnet job-stream success claim.
