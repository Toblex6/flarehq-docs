## Mainnet status — November 16, 2026

FlareHQ production reports Arc mainnet, chain `5042`. Arc's CCTP domain is `26`; Bridge Kit identifies the destination as `Arc`.

An earlier unauthenticated production POST to `/api/cctp/transfer` returned `401` requiring sign-in; later HTTP GET probes returned `503`. The production discrepancy remains unresolved. A successful mainnet bridge was **not verified** in this readiness run.

## Before transferring

The Circle server-authorized path requires operator-verified `CCTP_MAINNET_SOURCES_JSON` and `CCTP_MAINNET_ARC_CHAIN_ID=Arc`. It does not provision source-chain wallets: the current Circle consumer account rejects non-Arc sources with `BRIDGE_SOURCE_UNSUPPORTED`. A configured source list does not remove this account-binding limitation.

External-wallet bridging uses a separate flow with supported source funds/gas and a server-resolved linked Arc destination. Sign in, inspect the supported source and destination `Arc`, review amount/fees and authorize through the existing wallet flow only when your wallet and binding are usable. Do not use an unrelated wallet or testnet funds to bypass unavailable access.

## Verify completion

Keep the source burn and destination mint transaction hashes. Confirm the destination wallet, credited amount and successful receipt on [Arc Explorer](https://explorer.arc.io). Operators can inspect the corresponding intent and stage records with `scripts/inspect-bridge-attempt.mjs` in the application repository.

CCTP uses native burn-and-mint transfers. Fees can affect the received amount; do not assume every quoted amount arrives without deductions.
