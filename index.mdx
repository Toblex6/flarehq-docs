## How FlareHQ works

FlareHQ connects your application to Arc testnet through a simple REST API and JavaScript SDK. Payments settle in USDC using Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets — no private key management required on your end.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Sign up at [flarehq.xyz](https://flarehq.xyz) and verify your email to activate your merchant account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API keys">
    From the [Developer Dashboard](https://flarehq.xyz/developer), generate a Secret Key (`fhq_sec_...`) and Publishable Key (`fhq_pub_...`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the SDK">
    Add `@flarehq/sdk` to your project and initialize the client with your API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start accepting payments">
    Create a checkout session, set up an x402 paywall, or deploy a payment stream — all through the same unified SDK.
  </Step>
</Steps>
