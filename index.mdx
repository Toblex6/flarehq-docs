FlareHQ provides checkout, payment links and agent tooling on Arc. **Checkout is non-custodial: the customer pays directly to the merchant wallet; FlareHQ never holds checkout funds.** Circle Developer-Controlled wallets are a separate, server-authorized wallet service.

<Warning>
  **Mainnet readiness — November 19, 2026.** Production uses Arc Mainnet, chain `5042`. Authenticated requests and merchant link creation passed; hosted/embed rendering and historical settlement evidence were checked. A new checkout payment/replay needs explicit signer approval. Base→Arc lacks a funded signer, linked destination and live estimate. Escrow and ERC-8183 job actions remain unavailable; job streams require ERC-8183 and a stream contract, and x402 stays disabled. The assistant spent **0 USDC**; swaps were excluded. See [current evidence](/payments/availability).
</Warning>

Arc Testnet, chain `5042002`, is a separate development environment. [Get Test Tokens](https://faucet.circle.com) there; do not use faucet tokens for production.
