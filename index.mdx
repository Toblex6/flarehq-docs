FlareHQ provides checkout, payment links and agent tooling on Arc. **Checkout is non-custodial: the customer pays directly to the merchant wallet; FlareHQ never holds checkout funds.** Circle Developer-Controlled wallets are a separate, server-authorized wallet service.

<Warning>
  **Mainnet availability.** Production uses Arc Mainnet, chain `5042`. Checkout, payment links and platform fees are live, and the ArcFlareStream contract is deployed. Bridging is not yet proven end to end; ERC-8183 jobs, escrow and job streams are unavailable; x402 is disabled. See [mainnet availability](/payments/availability).
</Warning>

Arc Testnet, chain `5042002`, is a separate development environment. [Get Test Tokens](https://faucet.circle.com) there; do not use faucet tokens for production.
